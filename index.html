Glub Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Glub is an aquarium management application developed by HS Works ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information (Optional)

If you sign in with Google, we receive the following from your Google account:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL

This information is used solely for authentication and displaying your profile within the app. You can use Glub as a guest without providing any personal information.

2.2 User-Generated Aquarium Data

The app stores data you voluntarily enter:

  • Tank information: name, size, type, equipment details, notes
  • Fish inventory: species names, quantities, dates
  • Plant inventory: plant names, quantities, dates
  • Water parameters: pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, temperature, GH, KH readings
  • Reminders: maintenance schedules and due dates
  • Photos: tank photos taken via camera or selected from gallery

This data is stored locally on your device. If you sign in, it is additionally synced to our cloud service for backup and cross-device access. Photos are never uploaded — they remain on your device only.

2.3 Automatically Collected Data

App usage events (screens viewed, features used, in-app species search terms), a pseudonymous app user ID (our internal account ID, not your name or email), whether you use guest mode or a signed-in account, premium status, a random app-installation ID, app language, app version, device model, OS version Firebase Analytics (Google) Understanding which features are used, improving the app, and measuring the effectiveness of our own advertising campaigns (for example, app installs from Google Ads)

Analytics data is used only in aggregate to improve Glub and to measure the effectiveness of our own advertising campaigns (for example, app installs from Google Ads). We do not sell it and we do not use it to identify you personally. Google's services listed above (Firebase and AdMob) may derive an approximate location, such as your country or city, from your IP address. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, web browsing history, or other sensitive data.

To deliver the reminder notifications you set up, the titles and texts of your reminders are stored on our server. To deliver a reminder, our server sends its title, its text and a small amount of technical data (which screen to open, and the reminder ID and type) to Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which passes the notification on to your device. Stored reminders are kept while your account is active and are deleted with your account.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality
  • To sync your aquarium data across devices when signed in
  • To display advertisements in the free version
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and bugs
  • To authenticate your identity when signing in
  • To understand which features are used, to improve the app, and to measure the effectiveness of our own advertising campaigns (for example, app installs from Google Ads)
  • To send the reminder notifications you set up
  • To turn app features on or off and adjust ad frequency

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your aquarium data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. If you sign in, data is additionally synced to Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure). All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption in transit. Supabase enforces row-level security ensuring users can only access their own data.

5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In: Authentication — Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: Cloud data storage and sync — Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertisement display and, in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, the consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) for personalized ads, which also controls data collection by Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting — Firebase Privacy
  • Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Remote Config (Google): App analytics (including measuring our own advertising campaigns), push notifications and configuration — Google Privacy Policy

7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Use the app without an account: Guest mode requires no personal information
  • Delete your account and all data: Use the "Delete Account" button in Settings, or email us. All data is permanently removed from our servers immediately
  • Export your data: Use the Export feature in the app to download your aquarium data as JSON
  • Opt out of personalized ads: In regions where consent is required (e.g. EEA/UK/Switzerland), you can accept or decline personalized ads in the consent form shown by the app. In those regions the same choice also controls whether Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collect data, and you can change it at any time under Settings > Privacy Preferences. You can also adjust your advertising preferences in your device settings

For detailed instructions on account deletion, visit our Account Deletion page.

8. Data Retention

  • Account and aquarium data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Upon account deletion, all user data is removed from our servers immediately
  • Crash reports are not linked to your account
  • Analytics data is tied to a pseudonymous app user ID (the internal ID of your account) and to a random app-installation ID, never to your name or email. When you delete your account, only the user ID is deleted from our servers, because the installation ID is created by Firebase and is not part of your account data. After that, we can no longer connect the analytics data to you
  • Local data on your device is not automatically deleted — uninstall the app or clear app data to remove it

9. Children's Privacy

Glub is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
