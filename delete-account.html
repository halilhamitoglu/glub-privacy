Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Account

You can delete your Glub account and all associated data at any time. This page explains what happens when you delete your account and how to do it.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: From the App

  1. Open Glub and go to Settings (gear icon in the bottom navigation)
  2. Find the "Delete Account" button below your account info
  3. Confirm the deletion in the dialog
  4. Your account and all data will be deleted immediately

Option 2: Via Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to halilhamitoglu100@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Glub Account". Include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 business days.

What Data Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our servers:

  • Account information — your name, email, and profile
  • Tank data — all your aquarium tanks and their settings
  • Fish inventory — all fish records across all tanks
  • Plant inventory — all plant records across all tanks
  • Water parameters — all water test history and measurements
  • Reminders — all scheduled maintenance reminders

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Please export your data before deleting your account if you want to keep a copy.

Data Retention

Upon account deletion:

  • All user data is deleted from our servers immediately
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are not linked to your account. App analytics data (Firebase Analytics) is linked to your account only through a pseudonymous ID that is deleted from our servers with your account, so we can no longer connect it to you
  • Data stored locally on your device is not automatically deleted — you can uninstall the app or clear app data to remove it
